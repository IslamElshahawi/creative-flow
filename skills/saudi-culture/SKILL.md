# Saudi cultural direction

Use this for the cultural texture of a Saudi-facing ad. It is desk research,
not a legal clearance or a substitute for a Saudi reviewer. Verify current
seasonal timing and any regulated category separately.

1. Read the audience, brand, product and scene purpose. Identify which cultural
   details the route depends on: people, clothing, place, objects, gestures,
   humour or season.
2. Load only the relevant part of `references/field-guide.md`: wardrobe (§1),
   casting (§2), hospitality (§3), daily rhythm (§4), humour (§5), common
   foreign-made tells (§6) or regional hypotheses (§7).
3. Write a scene spec naming who appears, what they wear, where and when the
   scene happens, and how each visible object is used. Mark unsourced
   assumptions as hypotheses.
4. Show the concept and actual frames to a Saudi reviewer when the route
   depends on a cultural inference. Record the reviewer’s corrections and
   update the approved scene spec.

The review is complete when those scene choices are explicit, visible details
match the spec, and material cultural inferences have a recorded review.
Check current local rules and product-category requirements before release.
