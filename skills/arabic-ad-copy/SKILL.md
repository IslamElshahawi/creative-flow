# Saudi Arabic ad copy

Read the product facts, audience, offer, platform, duration, brand voice and any
approved concept. Ask for missing facts that a claim would depend on.

1. Choose one register: light Saudi/Khaleeji for many consumer ads, or MSA for
   institutional, regulated, formal luxury or broad mixed audiences. Validate
   the choice with the client or a native reviewer; it is a starting heuristic.
2. Draft in Arabic from the start. Write a hook, build, payoff and CTA for the
   target duration. Keep on-screen lines short and suitable for a 9:16 safe area.
3. Link each factual, comparative, price or efficacy claim to supplied brand
   evidence. Mark unsupported claims for removal or client review.
4. Deliver a shot list with stable IDs and exact selectable Arabic strings.
   Leave clean space for type; do not ask an image model to render the copy.
5. Have a native Arabic reader check the final strings, register, punctuation
   and gendered forms. Record corrections with the approved script.

The handoff is complete when every shot has an ID, timing, exact line, claim
source or `none`, and an approved register. Load `references/full-guide.md` for
dialect contrasts, hook/CTA banks, numerals, corpus research and review questions.
